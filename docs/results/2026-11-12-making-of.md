## Machine

- This PC: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Ti, driver 32.0.16.2101, Windows 11 Pro.
- Display: 1920 x 1080 at 240 Hz (Win32_VideoController CurrentRefreshRate 240), so one refresh is 4.17 ms.
- Browser: Chromium 158.0.8127.18 (Playwright 1.65.0, channel chromium), headed, 1280 x 800, a device pixel ratio of 1.

## Frame times

Frame times are read in the page from requestAnimationFrame: 3 s of warm-up, then every interval of a 10 s window (e2e/gpu/frame-times.ts). requestAnimationFrame never runs faster than the display, so a median on the display's refresh interval is that interval, not the GPU's cost. Every median below is 4.20 ms, the 240 Hz refresh rounded to 0.1 ms: the frame fits inside one refresh on both GPU tiers, and its real cost is lower than this test can see. The copy says so: "Under 4.2 ms, capped by a 240 Hz display". Timing the GPU itself needs timestamp queries, which WebGPU has and WebGL2 does not.
